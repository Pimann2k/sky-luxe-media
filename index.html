Questions

Common questions

How do quotes work?
Every project is quoted individually based on the location, coverage, shoot duration, deliverables and any drone requirements. Send through the project details and SkyLuxe will recommend a suitable scope.
How quickly are photos and videos delivered?
Edited galleries are delivered online within 24–48 hours of the shoot — fast enough to keep your listing or campaign momentum.
Do you offer aerial photography?
Yes. Aerial photography is available where it suits the project. SkyLuxe Media is CASA-accredited and completes the required airspace, weather and site-safety checks before drone work.
What is concept work?
Some portfolio items are concept work created to demonstrate visual direction, branding and cinematic storytelling. These are clearly labelled and are never presented as completed client projects or used to misrepresent actual property features or real locations.
Do you do ground-level photos as well as drone?
Yes. Aerial and ground-level photography can be combined depending on the project, giving you a complete visual set for listings, websites and social media.
Do you photograph events?
Yes. Event photography is available for celebrations, engagements, corporate functions, community events and venue-based occasions. Coverage is tailored to the event length, location and deliverables you need.
What areas do you service?
Greater Brisbane and surrounding South East Queensland. Travel further afield can be arranged — just mention the location in your enquiry.
